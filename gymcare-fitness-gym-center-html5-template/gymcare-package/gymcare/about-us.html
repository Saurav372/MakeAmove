We are dedicated to helping you live a stronger, healthier, and confident life.

Hi, I am Kunal — founder of Make A Move. As an Indian National Ultimate Frisbee professional athlete, I spent years refining athletic performance under rigorous international standards. But when I transitioned into remote coaching, I realized the vast majority of people were being failed by modern fitness culture.

Endless 90-minute workouts, exhausting gym commutes, and miserable calorie-deprivation diets only lead to burnout. Most desk workers, tech professionals, and parents don’t need more exhaustion — they need sustainable clarity.

"No extreme diets. No 90-minute workouts. Just 20–40 minute progressive overload sessions, workday mobility snacks, and real habit anchors that stick for life."
