BUSY PRO EXPRESS CASE STUDY

Anita — Product Manager, Bangalore

4 kg down • 7 cm off waist • 10-week streak
Shifted from sporadic HIIT classes to structured 30-minute strength sessions between sprint reviews.

The Initial Challenge

As a lead product manager at a fast-growing tech company, Anita worked 50+ hours a week with frequent interstate travel. She had spent two years cycling through fitness apps, random HIIT classes, and sporadic weekend runs. When work deadlines intensified, her workouts completely disappeared, leaving her feeling physically exhausted and mentally drained by 3:00 PM every day.

The Coaching Strategy

  • 30-Minute Calendar Blocks: We replaced 60-minute gym visits with three 30-minute compound lifting blocks scheduled into Google Calendar during non-meeting lunch windows.
  • Hotel-Room Travel Template: Created a zero-equipment bodyweight + mini-band alternative ready before every flight, preventing the usual travel slump.
  • Pre-Flight Protein Buffer: Established a simple rule: grab Greek yogurt, paneer, or whey before boarding flights to prevent reliance on airport carb snacks.
  • Asynchronous Video Form Checks: Anita uploaded 15-second smartphone clips of her Romanian deadlifts and goblet squats; Coach Kunal corrected hip hinge depth within 24 hours.
