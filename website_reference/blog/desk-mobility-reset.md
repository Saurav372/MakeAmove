# 10-Minute Desk Mobility Reset: Relieve Neck, Spine, and Hip Stiffness Between Calls

**Target Audience:** Desk athletes, remote workers, hybrid professionals  
**Program Track:** Posture Reset  
**Estimated Read Time:** 4 minutes  

---

## The Cost of the "Desk Slump"

Sitting at a laptop for 8 to 10 hours a day doesn't just make you feel sluggish—it systematically locks down three key joints:
1. **Thoracic Spine (Mid-Back):** Slumps forward, reducing shoulder mobility and compressing breathing mechanics.
2. **Hip Flexors:** Remain in a shortened position for hours, contributing directly to anterior pelvic tilt and lower back fatigue.
3. **Neck & Upper Trapezius:** Bears the constant strain of forward-head posture from looking at screens.

You don’t need an hour-long yoga class to undo this. Performing this **10-minute sequence** between Zoom calls or right after lunch restores blood flow and relieves joint tension.

---

## The 4-Move Routine (No Equipment Required)

Perform these moves consecutively. Repeat for 2 total rounds.

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  1. Seated / Kneeling Thoracic Rotations  ->  8 reps / side       |
|  2. Half-Kneeling Hip Flexor + Reach      ->  30s hold / side     |
|  3. Wall Scapular Slides                  ->  10 slow reps        |
|  4. Chin Tucks with Isometric Hold        ->  5 reps (5s hold)    |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Move 1: Half-Kneeling Hip Flexor + Overhead Reach
- **How to do it:** Drop one knee onto a cushion or mat. Squeeze the glute on the rear leg to push your hips slightly forward until you feel a gentle stretch in the front of your hip. Raise the same-side arm overhead and lean gently toward the opposite side.
- **Coach Cue:** *"Tuck your tailbone under (posterior tilt) before lunging. Never hyperextend your lower back."*

### Move 2: Seated / Quadruped Thoracic Opener
- **How to do it:** On hands and knees (or seated tall in your desk chair), place one hand behind your head. Rotate your elbow down toward the opposite forearm, then exhale as you peel your chest open toward the ceiling.
- **Coach Cue:** *"Follow your elbow with your eyes. Move only from your ribcage, not your hips."*

### Move 3: Wall Scapular Slides (W to Y)
- **How to do it:** Stand with your heels, glutes, upper back, and back of your head flush against a wall. Press your elbows and wrists against the wall in a 'W' shape, then glide your hands up into a 'Y' without arching your spine.
- **Coach Cue:** *"Keep your ribs pinned down. If your wrists peel off the wall, stop where your mobility allows."*

### Move 4: Seated Chin Tucks
- **How to do it:** Sit tall in your chair. Gently pull your chin straight back, as if making a subtle double chin. Hold for 5 seconds, release, and repeat.
- **Coach Cue:** *"Do not tilt your head down; slide your head directly backwards like a drawer closing."*

## How to Make It Stick: The Calendar Habit Hack

Don't wait until your back flares up at 6:00 PM. **Attach this routine to an existing anchor:**
- Place a recurring 10-minute block on your Google Calendar at 2:00 PM titled *"Posture Reset"*.
- Keep resistance bands by your desk as a visual cue.
