## The Problem: Airport Buffets and Meeting Snacks

When you’re juggling back-to-back presentations, cross-country flights, or 12-hour workdays, nutrition is usually the first casualty. Most professionals don't overeat out of lack of willpower—they overeat because their meals are 80% refined carbohydrates, leading to constant blood sugar spikes, energy crashes, and intense cravings by 4:00 PM.

Hitting your daily protein target (typically **1.4g to 2.0g per kilogram of body weight**) keeps you satiated, preserves lean muscle tissue, and eliminates late-night cravings.

---

## 4 Effortless Protein Hacks for Travel Days

### 1. The "Pre-Flight Greek Yogurt / Paneer / Whey" Buffer
Never board a plane on an empty stomach expecting healthy options in transit:
- Grab a single-serve Greek yogurt (15–20g protein) or roasted edamame/soya nuts at the airport lounge.
- Carry 2 pre-measured scoops of whey isolate or plant protein in a dry shaker bottle. Just ask flight attendants for water or cold milk.

### 2. The Restaurant "Double Protein" Rule
When dining with clients or ordering via food delivery apps:
- Always scan the menu for the protein anchor first (grilled chicken breast, eggs/omelette, tofu, paneer, fish, or lentils/dal).
- **The Secret:** Order an extra side of protein. Ask the server: *"Can I get an extra grilled chicken breast on the side?"* or *"Can you double the paneer/egg portion?"*

### 3. Smart Portable Snacks (Desk & Hotel Mini-Fridge)
Keep these stashed in your laptop bag or hotel room:
- Ready-to-drink protein shakes
- Roasted chickpeas / chana
- Boiled eggs (available at almost all hotel breakfast buffets)
- String cheese or cottage cheese cups
