# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** November 2026  
**Website:** [makeamove.fit](https://makeamove.fit/)  
**Entity:** Make A Move  

At **Make A Move**, accessible from `https://makeamove.fit`, the privacy of our visitors and clients is of utmost importance. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and how we use, protect, and handle it.

---

### 1. Information We Collect
When you interact with Make A Move, we may collect the following personal and health-related data:
- **Contact Details:** Full name, email address, phone number / WhatsApp contact details.
- **Fitness & Lifestyle Intake Data:** Primary fitness goals, time availability, movement limitations, past surgeries, injury histories, and current activity levels submitted via our intake form (`/book`).
- **Coaching & Check-In Information:** Progress tracking photos, body circumference measurements, workout logs, lifting videos submitted for form review, and habit scores.
- **Payment & Transaction Details:** Transaction IDs and confirmation receipts (note: credit card and banking details are processed directly by third-party payment gateways like Razorpay/UPI and are never stored on our servers).

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information exclusively to:
- Deliver personalized online personal training, mobility plans, and habit tracking.
- Conduct weekly check-ins, send workout feedback, and communicate through WhatsApp, email, or video calls.
- Respond to inquiries, support requests, and scheduling requests.
- Maintain client records and track fitness progress over time.
- Periodically send relevant service updates, fitness guides, or coaching announcements (you may opt out at any time).

---

### 3. Client Confidentiality & Progress Photos
- Client check-in photos, video clips, and metrics are strictly confidential.
- We **never publish, share, or market client transformation photos or testimonials** without explicit prior written consent. When client case studies are featured, identifying details are anonymized or modified upon request.

---

### 4. Third-Party Service Providers
We may employ reputable third-party services to facilitate coaching operations:
- **Form Submission:** FormSubmit.co for routing website inquiries.
- **Communication & Storage:** Google Workspace (Google Meet, Google Sheets, Google Drive), WhatsApp (Meta), and Zoom.
- **Payment Processing:** Secure payment gateways (e.g. Razorpay, UPI).

These third parties only have access to information necessary to perform their functional tasks and are obligated not to disclose or use it for other purposes.

---

### 5. Data Retention & Your Rights
You retain full control over your personal data:
- You may request a copy of your stored training logs and intake records at any time.
- You may request the prompt deletion of your personal data, form videos, and progress photos upon concluding your coaching program.
