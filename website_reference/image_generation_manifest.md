## 1. Asset Manifest Specifications

All photography prompts below have been engineered for mid-to-high end commercial AI image generators (e.g., Midjourney v6, Imagen 3, DALL-E 3) with precise instructions regarding:
- **Subject Demographics & Representation:** South Asian / Indian tech professionals, corporate desk workers, and Coach Kunal representation.
- **Lighting & Atmosphere:** Soft morning window light, high-end commercial gym loft, warm minimalist home office, and architectural depth.
- **Composition & Camera Settings:** 85mm portrait / 50mm normal / 35mm environmental lenses, shallow depth of field (f/1.8 - f/2.8), realistic skin texture without plastic smoothing.
- **Strict Negative Constraints:** Zero distorted anatomy, no extra limbs, no cartoon/CGI aesthetic, no text/watermarks, no unnatural bodybuilder proportions.

## 3. Image Optimization & Delivery Guidelines

1. **Resolution & Compression:**
   - Deliver generated assets in high-quality WebP or JPEG compressed to 80-85% quality to maintain sub-200KB file sizes for heroes, and sub-80KB for cards.
2. **Color Palette Alignment:**
   - Anchor tones around Make A Move's primary brand accents: Matte Charcoal/Black (`#111111`, `#181818`), Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`), and Electric Lime Accent (`#DEFB02`).
3. **Asset Placement:**
   - Save all generated assets directly into their target paths within `gymcare-fitness-gym-center-html5-template/gymcare-package/gymcare/image/`.
