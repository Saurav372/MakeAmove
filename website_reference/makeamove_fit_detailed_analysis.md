## 1. Executive Summary & Brand Positioning

**Make A Move** (`makeamove.fit`) is an online fitness coaching service founded by **Coach Kunal**, an Indian Ultimate Frisbee national/professional athlete who has delivered over 1,000+ 1-on-1 online sessions.

### Core Positioning
- **Core Target:** Desk workers, WFH software/tech professionals, product managers, corporate travelers, beginners, and postpartum mothers who struggle with gym culture, time scarcity, or recurring desk-slump pain.
- **Value Proposition:** *"Confused by workouts? Short on time? Build a plan you will actually stick to."*
- **Philosophy:** Rejecting 90-minute punishing workouts and crash dieting. Instead, delivering 20–40 minute progressive overload sessions, 10-minute workday mobility snacks, habit anchors, and rigorous human accountability.
- **Commercial Model:** High-touch async and sync coaching priced between ₹10,000/mo ($100) and ₹67,000/12 weeks ($700).

## 6. Strategic Findings, CRO Gaps & Recommendations

### 1. High-Priority Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)
- **Automate Booking via Cal.com / Calendly:** The `/book` page promises: *"We confirm your slot and send a calendar invite with Google Meet details."* However, the form is an asynchronous email submission. Replacing or pairing this with an embedded Calendly or Cal.com widget allows motivated prospects to self-book instantly, cutting lead drop-off significantly.
- **Add Visual Proof to `/results`:** The case studies (Anita, Rahul, Priya) are well-written but purely text. Adding screenshots of workout check-ins, WhatsApp message clips, habit streak dashboards, or posture before/after diagrams (with client identities protected) would multiply credibility.

### 2. Trust, Credibility & Compliance
- **Add Essential Legal Disclaimers:** The intake form asks for medical history and injury records. Make A Move must link a **Health/Medical Disclaimer**, **Privacy Policy**, and **Terms of Service** in the footer to limit legal liability and satisfy payment processor requirements.
- **Showcase Social Media Profiles:** Coach Kunal is an Ultimate Frisbee athlete, but the site provides zero links to Instagram, LinkedIn, or YouTube. Adding active social handles creates transparency and allows prospects to verify his expertise.

### 3. Commercial & Content Strategy
- **Publish the 5 Blog Articles:** The topics chosen (*Squat Form*, *Desk Mobility*, *Travel Protein*, *Habit Stacking*, *Postpartum Core*) are high-intent search queries. Publishing complete 1,000–1,500 word guides will turn `/blog` into an organic inbound client funnel.
- **Pricing Framing:** The 12-week *Consistency* plan (₹67,000 / $700) is marked *"Most popular"*. Consider offering split payments (e.g., 2 or 3 monthly installments) directly on the pricing card to lower price resistance.
